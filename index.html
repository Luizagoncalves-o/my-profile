Bonjour, I'm Luiza!

Brazilian girl, 20 years old, currently living my Parisian chapter.

I'm a Business student from Brazil, spending a semester abroad in Paris. I'm passionate about fashion, traveling, discovering new places, and spending quality time with my family.

As a true Gemini, I'm curious about almost everything! I love getting lost in a good book, watching movies, and finding inspiration in the little things.

I'm also learning French, because one semester in Paris simply isn't enough. Hopefully, one day, I'll get to call this beautiful city home for a little longer.

Discover Vogue France

My Favorite Fashion Destination

One of my favorite websites is Vogue France . It's my go-to place to keep up with the latest fashion shows, discover new designers, and explore trends from around the world. I especially love its editorials and the inspiration they bring to my personal style. For someone who loves fashion as much as I do, it's the perfect place to stay inspired and connected to the industry.

Fashion Is My Love Language

Fashion has always been one of my favorite ways to express myself. I love how clothes can tell a story, reflect a mood, and make someone feel completely themselves.

I also work as a model from time to time, which gives me the opportunity to experience the fashion world from a different perspective. Between university classes and modeling, I'm constantly discovering new ways to explore my creative side.

A Dream in the Making

One day, I dream of building something of my own in the fashion industry.

I believe there's still so much for me to learn, and I'd love to gain more experience and understand the industry beyond what we see on the runway.

My goal is to bring together my passion and experience in fashion with everything I'm learning in Business school. Hopefully, I'll turn these two worlds into something that's entirely my own.

Between Books & the Big Screen

When I'm not thinking about fashion, you'll probably find me reading a book, watching a movie, or getting completely invested in a TV series.

My all-time favorite show is One Tree Hill. What I love most about a good series is watching the characters evolve: understanding their choices, seeing how relationships change, and noticing how much people can grow from one season to the next.

For me, the best stories are the ones that stay with you long after they're over.
